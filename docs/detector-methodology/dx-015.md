# DX-015: Partition storage skew

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Evaluates storage imbalance among physical partitions of provisioned containers.
Azure Monitor `PhysicalPartitionSizeInfo` is split by `PhysicalPartitionId`; each partition's
largest non-null size in the window is used. Production requests seven days of hourly
samples. A known resource age below one day abstains. No Log Analytics is required.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `min_busiest_bytes` | `1073741824.0` |
| `balance_floor` | `0.7` |
| `high_pct_of_limit` | `80.0` |
| `medium_pct_of_limit` | `50.0` |

At least two partitions must be observed. The largest must hold at least 1 GiB and the
smallest/largest size ratio must be strictly below 0.7. Max/mean is descriptive, not a gate.
Using a 50 GiB physical-partition reference, the finding is High at 40 GiB, Medium at
25 GiB, otherwise Low. The size boundaries are inclusive after the imbalance gates.

## Evidence and limits

10/7 GiB is balanced; 10/6.9 GiB is skewed. For a skewed pair, 24.9/1 GiB is Low,
25/1 is Medium and 40/1 is High. A largest partition below 1 GiB is too small.
No usable size evidence or a known young resource abstains; failed reads are errors.

These are physical sizes, not measurements of individual logical keys. A physical partition
approaching 50 GiB does not prove a logical key has reached its limit or that writes will fail:
physical partitions can split. Largest sizes can come from different timestamps, and splits
can complicate interpretation. More RU/s or autoscale does not directly redistribute stored data.

## Remediation and references

Use
[`partition-high-cardinality`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/partition-high-cardinality.md)
to investigate distribution and key design. Obtain logical-key evidence before attributing
an oversized key or selecting a migration. CODA does not re-key or move data.
