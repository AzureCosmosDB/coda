# DX-015: Partition storage skew

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Log setup and pre-checks](../detections/log-analytics-readiness.md)

## Data and scope

DX-015 checks dedicated provisioned containers. It always compares physical storage using
Azure Monitor. If the account exports the **PartitionKeyStatistics** log category to Log
Analytics in **resource-specific** mode, CODA automatically checks `CDBPartitionKeyStatistics`
as well. No extra scan option, physical imbalance, growth history or minimum container age
is required. CODA does not enable logging. New settings collect future records, not past ones.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `min_busiest_bytes` | `1073741824.0` |
| `balance_floor` | `0.7` |

**Physical observation:** compare identified physical partitions in the same region and
hour over the seven-day lookback. The largest must be at least 1 GiB and the smallest
strictly below 70% of the largest. Report their sizes, region and observation time with
no severity. For example, 40 GiB versus 4 GiB warrants checking the largest logical
partitions, not a re-key instruction. A physical partition can split. A missing sample
is not zero, and incomplete regional inventory cannot establish a balanced container.

**Logical check:** use the latest sampled size per full logical key in this database and
container, then the largest recent size. Replicas and repeated samples are not summed.
Report the sample timestamp, approximate size and headroom against the **default 20 GiB**
limit. Only samples from the last 24 hours of the observation window qualify for grading.
Key values are not returned in reports.

| Latest sampled key size | Report |
|---|---|
| Up to and including 14 GiB | Measured size, ungraded |
| Above 14 GiB but below 18 GiB | Ungraded early warning: monitor the key's size and growth |
| At least 18 GiB | **High**: verify remaining headroom and the applicable limit |

These are unrounded comparisons. The early warning follows the
[Microsoft Learn alert example](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cosmos-db/how-to-alert-on-logical-partition-key-storage-size).
High is a CODA policy, not proof of write failures or a forecast. A temporary limit
exception may apply. For hierarchical keys the limit is for the full key, not a prefix.

## Evidence and limits

The [table](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/azure-monitor/reference/tables/cdbpartitionkeystatistics)
samples outliers; keys below 1 GB may not be reported. No recent rows, stale samples, missing
tables, failed reads and unknown configuration do not mean every key is safe. If export
is off, the report explains how to enable it. If access is denied, check permissions rather
than recreating an existing setting. Either check can report its evidence even if the other
cannot complete.

## Remediation and references

A High finding links to
[`partition-high-cardinality`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/partition-high-cardinality.md)
for investigation. Verify the applicable limit and growth before a design change. CODA does
not expose literal partition keys, migrate data, or change account configuration.
