# DX-014: Serverless candidate

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

An account-level advisory for low, intermittent provisioned consumption. Already-serverless
accounts are not candidates. Production requests 30 days of account-total `TotalRequestUnits`
in hourly buckets, divides each total by bucket duration to obtain RU/s, and requires at least
24 non-null samples. No Log Analytics or per-container request scan is needed.

## Detection and severity

The account peak must be between 10 and 5,000 RU/s, inclusive, and average/peak must be
strictly below 0.1. The account-level 5,000 RU/s threshold is deliberately conservative;
it is not a measurement of every container's or partition's capacity.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `sporadic_ratio` | `0.1` |
| `peak_ceiling_ru` | `5000.0` |
| `peak_floor_ru` | `10.0` |
| `min_points` | `24` |

Matching accounts receive **Low** severity. Below the peak floor is idle/empty; above the
ceiling is too high; a ratio exactly 0.1 is steady, not a candidate.

## Evidence and limits

A 5,000 RU/s peak with sufficiently low average can qualify; 5,001 cannot. Fewer than
24 samples or no history are insufficient data. A service failure is an error.
Hourly bucket averages can hide shorter spikes; this is a candidate screen, not proof
that serverless can sustain the workload.

Validate current API/region support, shared-throughput restrictions, storage, partition-level
distribution, migration requirements and billing before acting. These platform constraints
are customer eligibility checks, not all independently measured gates in this detector.
No monetary saving or in-place provisioned-to-serverless conversion is promised.
