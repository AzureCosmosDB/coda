# DX-010: Uncontrolled ingestion

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Log prerequisites](../detections/log-analytics-readiness.md)

## Data and scope

Finds write-dominant throttling in a container. Production uses one day of account-resource
Log Analytics reads, preferring `CDBDataPlaneRequests5M` and supporting raw
`CDBDataPlaneRequests`. Aggregate inputs sum `RequestCount` and `TotalRequestCharge`.
Five-minute windows must use completed/aligned buckets.

Write operations are Create, Upsert, Replace, Delete, Patch, Batch and Execute.
The detector compares write RU to all RU and 429 requests to all requests.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `write_ru_pct_floor` | `80.0` |
| `throttle_rate_pct` | `10.0` |
| `severity_high_pct` | `20.0` |
| `min_total_requests` | `1000.0` |

At least 1,000 requests, at least 80% write RU and at least 10% server-side throttling
are required. Severity is High at a 429 rate of 20% or more, otherwise Medium.
With zero total RU, write share is zero, not a write-dominance finding.

## Evidence and limits

At 10,000 requests, 999 throttles fail the 10% gate; 1,000 meet it.
1,999 throttles yield Medium and 2,000 yield High if the other gates pass.
999 total requests are insufficient data even when the percentages are large.

Burst factor and dominant write user-agent are **supporting evidence, not gates**.
Burst factor is peak/average write RU over observed write buckets: one-minute for raw logs,
five-minute for aggregates. Quiet/missing buckets are not imputed, and these resolutions are
not interchangeable. SDK retries mean server-side 429 rates can differ from application errors.

No relevant records are insufficient data, unavailable capabilities identify setup gaps,
and failed queries remain query errors. A user-agent string is not proof of a connector or
an instruction to an agent. The finding is not proof that partition skew or capacity limits
are absent; DX-005 is a complementary infrastructure signal.
