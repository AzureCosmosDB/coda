# DX-001: Manual over-provisioning

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Finds manually provisioned throughput offers whose observed demand is low relative to their
allocation. An offer can belong to a dedicated container or a shared-throughput database;
do not count the same database offer once per member container. Autoscale uses DX-011.

Production requests 30 days of hourly Azure Monitor `NormalizedRUConsumption` maxima,
with ARM throughput configuration. At least 24 non-null samples are required. A known
resource age below seven days causes abstention. Requested history is not proof of complete history.

## Detection and severity

The utilization statistic is p99, using linearly interpolated percentiles. Bands are idle
below 1%, low from 1% to below 10%, moderate from 10% to below 30%, and otherwise OK.
A peak at or above 90% prevents an over-provisioning finding even when p99 is low.
Bands describe utilization, not severity.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `band_statistic` | `"p99"` |
| `idle_pct` | `1.0` |
| `low_pct` | `10.0` |
| `moderate_pct` | `30.0` |
| `min_ru` | `400` |
| `headroom` | `1.3` |
| `peak_saturation_pct` | `90.0` |

The target covers p99 demand with 30% headroom and the observed peak, rounds demand upward
to whole RU/s, respects the model's 400 RU/s floor, and never exceeds current throughput.
Recoverable capacity is current minus recommended RU/s. Actual service minimums can be higher;
the recommendation is not an authoritative throughput-update validation.

Severity is High at recoverable capacity of at least 5% of the scope's provisioned capacity,
Medium at least 1%, otherwise Low. Without a usable scope total, the fallbacks are 5,000
and 1,000 recoverable RU/s. An OK result has no finding severity.

## Evidence and limits

At 10,000 RU/s with constant 5% utilization, the target is 650 RU/s and recoverable capacity
is 9,350 RU/s. At the 400 RU/s floor there is no right-sizing recovery. No samples or fewer
than 24 samples are insufficient data, not a clean result. A failed metric read is an error.

Past peaks cannot predict future demand, seasonal jobs, or growth. A shared offer is one
capacity unit. Rollup can supersede right-sizing with an idle or mode-switch finding.
Optional report-level bill-derived savings require their own provenance; RU/s is not currency.
The threshold label does not certify fresh live validation on your account.

## Remediation and references

Review the evidence, service floors and workload headroom before changing throughput.
The emitted Agent Kit rule is
[`throughput-right-size`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/throughput-right-size.md).
CODA does not resize anything.
