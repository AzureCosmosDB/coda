# DX-007: Shard-key misalignment

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Shared fan-out signal](dx-002.md)

## Data and scope

Escalates widespread fan-out into a partition-key/access-pattern review for a container,
including serverless containers. It reuses DX-002's one-day query-statistics/raw-request-log
signal and data requirements; there is no separate observation of the correct key.
Both `CDBQueryRuntimeStatistics` and raw `CDBDataPlaneRequests` are required.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `structural_pct` | `60.0` |
| `min_partitions` | `2` |

After the shared minimum-query/sufficiency checks, at least 60% of observed executions must
fan out, at least two partitions must be observed, and fan-out volume must reach DX-002's
0.1 query/s floor. The shape fan-out threshold is 1.5 partitions and the sample floor is 50.
There is no RU-charge gate.

Severity uses the same matrix as DX-002: 2-3 partitions are Low; 4-6 are Low below
1 fan-out query/s and Medium at/above it; 7+ are Medium below 1/s and High at/above it.
All share/rate decisions use unrounded measurements.

## Evidence and limits

5,995 fan-out executions out of 10,000 are 59.95%, below the structural gate even if
the displayed share rounds to 60.0%. In one hour, 359 fan-out executions do not reach
the rate floor. With seven observed partitions, 3,599/hour is Medium, not High.

Single-partition or sub-threshold workloads are not structural findings. Insufficient
query data or a failed shared query remain incomplete, not evidence that the key is aligned.
The top-shape/observed-partition limitations of DX-002 also apply.

Dominant fan-out suggests a design mismatch; it does not prove which key is correct,
identify literal source-code properties, or rule out intentional reporting queries.
When both detectors fire on the same unit, rollup supersedes DX-002 to avoid double counting.

## Remediation and references

Use
[`partition-rekey-migration`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/partition-rekey-migration.md)
to review access patterns and, if warranted, a new-container/backfill/cutover plan. Do not
make a migration decision from anonymized query shapes alone. CODA never migrates data.
