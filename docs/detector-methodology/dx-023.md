# DX-023: Reserved-capacity candidate

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

An account-level advisory from the pipeline's provisioned-throughput total and ARM serverless
capability. It uses a configuration snapshot, not historical bill stability or an inventory
of existing reservation coverage.

## Detection and severity

**Threshold status: proposal.**

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `MIN_BASELINE_RU` | `3000.0` |

Serverless is not applicable. A missing throughput total is unknown; below 3,000 RU/s
is below the proposed baseline. At or above 3,000 RU/s is a **Low** candidate, irrespective
of the account's absolute size. There is no Medium or High band.

## Evidence and limits

2,999 RU/s is not detected and 3,000 RU/s is a candidate. At 250,000 RU/s, the result is still a
**Low-severity reservation-review advisory**. "Low" describes the advisory's severity, not low
throughput or utilization, and it is not evidence that potential savings are small. Every
qualifying account receives the same Low severity; there are no size-based Medium/High bands.
An unavailable inventory is not proof of zero capacity.

Provisioned throughput is only a proxy for a sustained billable baseline. This detector
does not establish pay-as-you-go stability, normalized reservation units, existing coverage,
future utilization, or customer-specific eligibility. It does not claim that a reservation
is absent, compute a discount, or quantify monetary savings.

## Remediation and references

There is no mapped Agent Kit rule (`agent_kit_rule` is null). Review eligible usage,
existing reservations, negotiated rates and commitment risk in Cost Management and the
current reservation offering before considering a one- or three-year purchase.
CODA makes no purchase, commitment or billing change.
