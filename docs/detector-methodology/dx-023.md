# DX-023: Reserved-capacity candidate

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

An account-level prompt for verified provisioned throughput. An autoscale maximum is a
configured ceiling, not the throughput billed each hour.

## Detection and severity

**Threshold status: proposal.**

Show an **ungraded** Cost Management review regardless of size, with no age or usage-history
gate. Serverless is not applicable. A verified offer suffices even if the account total
is incomplete. Do not infer a provisioned account from unavailable inventory.

## Evidence and limits

A 400 RU/s manual offer and a 250,000 RU/s autoscale maximum both warrant the same review.
Neither establishes uncovered hourly usage, future demand, a purchase quantity, term, or savings.

## Remediation and references

There is no mapped Agent Kit rule (`agent_kit_rule` is null). Review eligible usage,
existing reservations, negotiated rates and commitment risk in Cost Management and the
current reservation offering before considering a one- or three-year purchase.
CODA makes no purchase, commitment or billing change.
