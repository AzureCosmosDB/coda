# DX-005: Global under-provisioning

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

A server-side HTTP 429 means a request was throttled. It does **not** by itself mean your
application failed, or that more capacity would help. CODA shows measured counts and rates
from the first reading, including during an open hour. Unknown resource age is not a reason
to discard these measurements. If a count has no matching denominator, the rate is unknown.

The scope is one manual or autoscale throughput offer: a dedicated container or a shared
database. For shared throughput, request counts must reconcile for the verified shared members;
dedicated siblings are excluded. No Log Analytics query is needed.

## Detection and severity

CODA checks seven days of completed UTC hours. **Two consecutive hours on that offer must
qualify independently.** In each, `TotalRequests` (Count) must show at least 1,000 requests
and at least 1% with `StatusCode=429`. In **every configured region**, the same-hour
`PhysicalPartitionCount` must match the identified `NormalizedRUConsumption` partition
maxima. The highest maximum must reach 90% and the lowest must reach 70%.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `throttle_rate_pct` | `1.0` |
| `saturation_pct` | `90.0` |
| `uniform_min_pct` | `70.0` |
| `severity_med_pct` | `5.0` |
| `severity_high_pct` | `20.0` |
| `min_total_requests` | `1000.0` |

Severity uses the lower of the two hours' 429 rates: High at 20%, Medium at 5%, otherwise
Low. Where multiple pairs qualify, the report shows the pair with the highest such rate,
then the latest in a tie. Inspect its actual dates and counts before acting.

**Example:** at 09:00–10:00 and 10:00–11:00 UTC, the same offer has 1,000 requests and
100 throttles in each hour. Both configured regions each report two partitions, at 95%
and 92%. That supports a Medium recommendation to **consider** more capacity for the
observed load. If a region is missing in the second hour, its cooler partition is at 20%,
or the 10:00 hour is still open, keep the 429 observation but withhold the increase.

## Evidence and limits

A quiet week cannot dilute a qualifying pair. Nor can a weekly average, two separated
busy hours, missing region names, or a gap *within* a witness hour establish one.
Hourly maxima are not proof that all partitions were simultaneously or continuously hot.
Skew can warrant checking DX-006 instead. Shared-pool contention, ingestion pressure,
and SDK retries may require different responses. Confirm the observed load is representative.

## Remediation and references

The relevant Agent Kit rule is
[`throughput-right-size`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/throughput-right-size.md)
