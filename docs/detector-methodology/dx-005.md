# DX-005: Global under-provisioning

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Evaluates provisioned manual/autoscale offers, including shared databases. It correlates
`TotalRequests` totals and 429 totals with `NormalizedRUConsumption` maxima split by
`PhysicalPartitionId`. Production uses seven days of hourly metrics; no Log Analytics is needed.
A known resource age below one day or fewer than 1,000 requests causes abstention.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `throttle_rate_pct` | `1.0` |
| `saturation_pct` | `90.0` |
| `uniform_min_pct` | `70.0` |
| `severity_med_pct` | `5.0` |
| `severity_high_pct` | `20.0` |
| `min_total_requests` | `1000.0` |

Compute each observed partition's p99 utilization. A finding requires a server-side 429 rate
of at least 1%, the hottest partition's p99 at least 90%, and the coolest at least 70%.
These inclusive gates distinguish broad pressure from one hot partition.

For a finding, 429 rates at least 20% are High, at least 5% are Medium, otherwise Low.
If the hottest is saturated but the coolest is below 70%, the verdict is `skew_suspected`,
not an under-provisioning finding; inspect DX-006 instead. Its intermediate verdict can carry
a severity even though it does not produce an actionable DX-005 finding.

## Evidence and limits

At 10,000 requests with 1,000 throttles, partition p99s of 100%/70% meet the global gates;
100%/69% indicate skew. A hottest p99 of 89% does not establish saturation.
Exactly 1,000 requests meet the volume floor; 999 do not.

SDK retries can hide server-side 429s from application error counts. The window does not
prove that all partitions were simultaneously saturated. Missing partition history limits
the interpretation; an absence of this finding is not proof that no throttling occurred.
Failed service reads remain errors. Raising throughput does not necessarily fix skew,
shared-pool contention, or excessive ingestion.

## Remediation and references

The pipeline uses
[`throughput-autoscale`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/throughput-autoscale.md)
for an autoscale offer and
[`throughput-right-size`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/throughput-right-size.md)
for manual throughput. Review the existing finding's exact rule and action: raise the autoscale
maximum versus provisioned RU/s as appropriate. CODA does not execute the change.
