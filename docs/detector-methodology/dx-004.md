# DX-004: Idle throughput

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Evaluates a provisioned throughput offer, including a dedicated container or a shared-database
offer, in manual or autoscale mode. Serverless has no provisioned reservation to recover.
An idle shared member does not make its entire shared database offer recoverable.

Production requests 30 days of `TotalRequestUnits` totals in five-minute buckets.
`NormalizedRUConsumption` is corroborating evidence, not the idle decision. ARM supplies the
offer and creation context. A known age below seven days causes abstention. No Log Analytics
is required.

## Detection and severity

The largest consumed-RU bucket must be at or below 50 RU. This is RU consumed per five-minute
bucket, not RU/s, utilization, a request count, or an average over the whole window.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `max_idle_peak_ru` | `50.0` |

Exactly 50 RU qualifies; 51 RU does not. One larger bucket protects an otherwise quiet workload.
Manual recovery is the full offer. The current autoscale estimate is 10% of its configured
maximum; this is a capacity estimate, not the complete bill or a platform minimum calculation.

Severity is High at at least 5% of scope capacity, Medium at at least 1%, otherwise Low.
Without a usable scope denominator, the fallbacks are 5,000 and 1,000 recoverable RU/s.

## Evidence and limits

A 1,000 RU/s manual offer peaking at 4 RU per bucket has 1,000 recoverable RU/s.
An autoscale maximum of 10,000 RU/s has a modeled idle floor of 1,000 RU/s.

Unlike most checks, this detector treats a **successful empty consumption series as zero**,
as well as explicit zero-valued samples. This is current behavior, not proof that telemetry
was complete or a resource is unused. Failed metric calls are errors, not empty measurements.
Review collection/retention and intentional standby or infrequent batch use before acting.
A known young resource abstains; an unavailable creation time does not establish its age.

Deleting data or applying TTL alone does not necessarily remove a throughput reservation.
Optional savings are separate report enrichment, and other right-sizing findings can be
superseded by this offer-level finding.
