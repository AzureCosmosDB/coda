# DX-021: Throttling by operation

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Account-level Azure Monitor `TotalRequests` by status, plus `OperationType` filtered to 429.
Production requests seven days at hourly grain. No Log Analytics is needed.
The check names the most frequent throttled operation and reports category shares;
it does not independently prove a root cause.

## Detection and severity

**Threshold status: proposal.**

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `MIN_TOTAL_REQUESTS` | `1000` |
| `THROTTLE_RATE_PCT` | `1.0` |
| `SEVERITY_MEDIUM_PCT` | `5.0` |
| `SEVERITY_HIGH_PCT` | `20.0` |

At least 1,000 requests, at least 1% 429s and a nonempty positive operation breakdown
are required. Severity is High at 20%, Medium at 5%, otherwise Low.
Dominant operation is selected by its individual 429 count; the category of that operation
is used for guidance, not necessarily the category with the largest combined count.

## Evidence and limits

At 10,000 requests, 99 throttles are below the gate, 100 are Low, 500 are Medium,
and 2,000 are High. The operation-share denominator is total 429s from the status signal.
Unknown operation names map to `other`.

Fewer than 1,000 requests abstain. Failed reads are errors. With no operation breakdown
the current classifier returns `not_throttling` even if status metrics contain 429s;
that token is **not proof that there were no throttles**. Check the request counts and
breakdown before interpreting coverage. Independent series can have different completeness.
Operation mix alone does not establish fan-out, hot keys, an ingestion implementation, or
per-application attribution.
