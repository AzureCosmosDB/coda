# DX-021: Throttling by operation

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Read account-level Azure Monitor `TotalRequests` split by status (Count) over seven days.
For observed HTTP 429s, read `TotalRequests` split by `OperationType`, filtered to 429,
for the **same account, scope, window and hourly granularity**. No Log Analytics is needed.

## Detection and severity

There is no rate floor, volume gate, or severity. Show the measured 429 count and rate
per affected interval even without another finding. The headline describes the interval
with the most 429s. Other affected intervals remain in the structured evidence.
Report operation counts and shares only if they sum exactly to that hour's status total.
Never reconcile one hour's surplus against another's shortfall.

## Evidence and limits

Five of 2,000 attempts return 429 (0.25%); two were reads and three were creates. Reads
received 40% of the throttles. This is a **share of all 429s**, not a throttle rate within
the read operation. If only two operation responses were returned, or all five appeared in
another hour, keep the 5/2,000 observation but mark operation attribution unavailable.
Do the same when the operation read fails.

## Remediation and references

No dominant operation, inferred cause or
operation-specific remedy follows from incomplete attribution. Even a reconciled recipient
breakdown does not identify which traffic caused the pressure or which clients failed.
