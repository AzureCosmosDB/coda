# DX-012: Manual-to-autoscale candidate

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Reviews dedicated manual-throughput containers for a bursty duty cycle. This is a mode-change
candidate, not a lower-fixed-throughput recommendation or quantified currency saving.
Production uses 30 days of hourly `NormalizedRUConsumption` maxima and ARM throughput.
It requires 24 non-null samples; a known age below seven days abstains.

## Detection and severity

All three inclusive conditions must hold: peak at least 40%, average at most 30%, and
peak/average at least 5. The ratio is zero when the average is non-positive.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `spiky_peak_floor` | `40.0` |
| `spiky_avg_ceiling` | `30.0` |
| `spiky_ratio` | `5.0` |

The finding is **Medium**, not capacity-materiality severity. Its suggested autoscale
maximum covers observed peak RU/s with 30% headroom, rounded upward to a multiple of
1,000 RU/s with a minimum of 1,000. Validate actual service minimums separately.

## Evidence and limits

Average 10% and peak 50% meet the ratio exactly; peak 49% does not. A peak of 39% is
below the floor even when the average is very low. A 10,000 RU/s manual offer with a 50%
peak yields a suggested 7,000 RU/s autoscale maximum.

No/fewer-than-24 samples or a known young container are insufficient data. A failed read
is an error. Historical burstiness is not a forecast. Price premiums, negotiated rates,
and actual hourly billing affect the business decision. This detector does not quantify
recoverable RU/s or guaranteed savings. Rollup can supersede a competing DX-001 finding.

Load
[`throughput-autoscale`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/throughput-autoscale.md)
and evaluate the conversion with the workload owner. CODA does not change capacity mode.
