# DX-006: Hot partition

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Finds uneven physical-partition utilization for provisioned offers, including shared databases.
It compares `NormalizedRUConsumption` **hourly maxima** for identified physical partitions
in the same region and hour. The normal lookback is seven days; it is **not** a waiting period
or a seven-day p99 test. A known new resource is queried starting no earlier than creation;
the first usable contrast is reported even before two full hours have elapsed.
`TotalRequests` (Count), with and without the 429 filter, supplies impact for the same offer
and timestamps. A shared database's owner-scoped skew remains valid if a member's counts
are unreadable. Never substitute a dedicated sibling's traffic.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `saturation_pct` | `90.0` |
| `uniform_min_pct` | `70.0` |
| `throttle_rate_pct` | `1.0` |
| `min_total_requests` | `1000` |

| What the measurements establish | Report |
|---|---|
| One partition reached at least 90% and another peaked **below** 70% in the same region and interval | **Observation only**; record the IDs, region, time and measurements. An open hour is never confirmation. |
| The **same hot partition** did this in **two consecutive completed hours** in the same region | **Medium**. The cooler sibling may differ between the hours. |
| The Medium condition holds, and **each of those two hours** independently has **at least 1,000 requests and at least 1% HTTP 429s** for the offer | **High**. No averaging across hours or borrowing 429s from another time. |

Missing request counts mean *unknown*, not zero; verified persistent skew still merits
Medium. No minimum request volume is required for the initial observation or for Medium.
The report selects the strongest supported pair in the lookback and shows its timestamps;
it does not assert that a historical incident is still occurring.

## Evidence and limits

Example: in West, A reaches 98% while B peaks at 20% in each of two adjacent completed hours.
If the offer has 20 of 2,000 requests throttled in the first hour and 30 of 2,000 in the
second, it is High. If instead only the second hour throttles, or one hour has 10 of 999
requests throttled, show the counts and keep Medium. If A is hot only in the first hour
and B only in the second, show a contrast without calling either partition persistently hot.
“Reached 98%” is an hourly maximum, not proof it stayed there for an hour. A single physical
partition cannot establish imbalance across partitions.

CODA checks an independent `PhysicalPartitionCount` for the same offer, region and window.
One usable series is not proof of one physical partition. A hot/cool pair observed on common
buckets within one region can yield a named, qualified finding even if another partition or
region is missing. Hot in one region and cool in another is not such a pair. Without a witness,
an incomplete region, missing count or changed topology yields `insufficient_data` rather than
a balanced or single-partition result. Coverage evidence separates expected, observed and usable.

The metric identifies physical-partition pressure, not a particular logical partition-key
value, which physical partition returned an HTTP 429, or a definitive cause in application code.
Investigate which keys consume the most RU before changing throughput or key design. Failed regional reads are identified in
coverage evidence, not interpreted as zero utilization.
Independent per-region/per-partition autoscale may change cost, but does not remove a hot
logical key's capacity limit. Hourly maxima do not establish the exact time of a 429.

## Remediation and references

Use the emitted
[`partition-high-cardinality`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/partition-high-cardinality.md)
rule to review key distribution. Re-keying requires a separately reviewed migration;
additional logical-key evidence is needed to name an offending key. CODA changes nothing.
