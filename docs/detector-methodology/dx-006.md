# DX-006: Hot partition

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Finds uneven physical-partition utilization for provisioned offers, including shared databases.
It reads `NormalizedRUConsumption` maxima split by `PhysicalPartitionId`, reducing each
observed series to p99. `TotalRequests` totals and 429 counts supply offer-level throttling.
Production uses seven days of hourly metrics and a one-day known-resource-age floor.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `saturation_pct` | `90.0` |
| `uniform_min_pct` | `70.0` |
| `throttle_rate_pct` | `1.0` |

At least two observed partitions, hottest p99 at least 90%, and coolest p99 **below** 70%
produce a hot-partition finding. Exactly 70% is uniform saturation, not this finding.
Max/mean skew ratio is evidence only; it is not the trigger.

Severity is **High** at a 429 rate of at least 1%, otherwise **Medium**, including zero
observed throttles. There is no Low band and no DX-005-style 1,000-request volume floor
in this detector.

## Evidence and limits

Partition p99s of 90%/69% meet the trigger; 89%/10% do not. 100%/70% is uniform.
With a matching distribution and 10,000 requests, 99 throttles remain Medium and
100 become High. No partition series or a known age below one day is insufficient data.
A single partition cannot establish imbalance across partitions.

The metric identifies physical-partition pressure, not a particular logical partition-key
value or a definitive cause in the application's code. A failed read is an error.
Independent per-region/per-partition autoscale may change cost, but does not remove a hot
logical key's capacity limit. Past p99s do not establish simultaneous load.

## Remediation and references

Use the emitted
[`partition-high-cardinality`](https://github.com/AzureCosmosDB/cosmosdb-agent-kit/blob/main/skills/cosmosdb-best-practices/rules/partition-high-cardinality.md)
rule to review key distribution. Re-keying requires a separately reviewed migration;
additional logical-key evidence is needed to name an offending key. CODA changes nothing.
