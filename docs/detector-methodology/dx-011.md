# DX-011: Autoscale maximum too high

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Evaluates autoscale throughput offers, including shared databases. The signal is
`AutoscaledRU` summed across physical partitions at each timestamp, expressed as a percentage
of the configured maximum. `NormalizedRUConsumption` alone has a different autoscale denominator.

Production requests 30 days at hourly grain and requires 24 non-null samples. A known
resource age below seven days abstains. ARM supplies the maximum; no Log Analytics is needed.

## Detection and severity

Use the **maximum**, not p99, percentage of configured maximum. Idle is below 1%, low is
from 1% to below 10%, moderate is from 10% to below 30%, otherwise OK. Exactly 10% is moderate.
The shared peak-saturation guard also makes peaks at or above 90% OK.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `band_statistic` | `"max"` |
| `idle_pct` | `1.0` |
| `low_pct` | `10.0` |
| `moderate_pct` | `30.0` |
| `AUTOSCALE_MIN_MAX_RU` | `1000` |
| `headroom` | `1.3` |
| `peak_saturation_pct` | `90.0` |

The target covers the observed peak with 30% headroom, never goes below the **1,000 RU/s
configurable maximum baseline**, and does not exceed the current maximum. At that baseline,
autoscale can scale down to **100 RU/s** when idle. The manual-throughput minimum of 400 RU/s
is a different constraint and is not used by this detector.

This remains an **analytical target**, not a service-ready setting. Accepted increments and
resource-specific minimum maxima can raise the actual target; storage, throughput history, and
shared-database constraints are not inferred by this detector. Verify service eligibility before
an update and reassess recovery using the eligible maximum.

Recoverable capacity is the reduction in the modeled billing floor:
`0.1 * current_max - 0.1 * recommended_max`, bounded below by zero.
It is **not** the full maximum reduction. Severity uses recoverable capacity: High at 5%
of scope capacity, Medium at 1%, otherwise Low, or absolute fallbacks of 5,000/1,000 RU/s.

## Evidence and limits

A 10,000 RU/s maximum with an observed 1,000 RU/s peak produces a 1,300 RU/s analytical target
and 870 recoverable RU/s (floors of 1,000 and 130). The 1,300 target is not rounded to a
service-accepted increment. A 4,000 maximum peaking at 400 produces a 1,000 target and
300 recoverable RU/s (floors of 400 and 100), not zero and not the full 3,000 maximum reduction.
With no scope denominator these examples are Low severity, despite their moderate utilization band.

At a 1,000 maximum with a 100 peak, the target remains 1,000 and recovery is zero. The unchanged
utilization-band policy can still report a Low finding; it does not imply a smaller eligible maximum.

Missing/insufficient samples and a known young resource abstain; access/query failures are
errors. Past provisioning does not guarantee future demand. Hourly billing reflects the highest
throughput actually scaled to in the hour, with a 10% minimum. The idle-floor estimate is not an
hour-by-hour bill simulation or guaranteed savings; any currency estimate must retain its separate
pricing provenance.
An idle finding may supersede this finding.
