# DX-013: Autoscale-to-manual candidate

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Reviews dedicated autoscale containers whose load stays high with little benefit from scaling
down. `AutoscaledRU` is summed across physical partitions and normalized to the configured
maximum; do not substitute utilization relative to currently scaled capacity.

Production uses a 30-day hourly window, at least 24 non-null samples and ARM configuration.
A known age below seven days abstains. No Log Analytics is needed.

## Detection and severity

Average utilization must be at least 66% of configured maximum and peak/average must be
at most 1.3. Both comparisons are inclusive. With zero average the computed ratio is zero,
but the average gate still prevents a finding.

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `steady_avg_floor` | `66.0` |
| `steady_ratio_ceiling` | `1.3` |

A matching duty cycle produces **Medium** severity. This is a qualitative pricing-mode
recommendation, not a capacity-materiality calculation or a measured monetary saving.

## Evidence and limits

Average 66% and peak 85% qualify. Average 65% fails the average gate; average 66% and
peak 90% fail the ratio gate. Missing/fewer-than-24 samples or a known young resource
are insufficient data. Failed telemetry reads are errors, not a clean result.

Evaluate actual rates, reservations, growth and future peaks before switching. The
classification cannot establish that manual capacity will always cover future load.
The suggested action is to assess a fixed level near sustained demand; it is not a
service-validated update payload.
