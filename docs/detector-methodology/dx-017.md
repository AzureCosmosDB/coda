# DX-017: Rapid storage growth

[Catalog](../detectors.md) | [Access and sign-in](../permissions.md)

## Data and scope

Projects storage growth for physical partitions of provisioned containers. Production requests
30 days of hourly `PhysicalPartitionSizeInfo` maxima split by `PhysicalPartitionId`.
Each series uses its last non-null size and a least-squares bytes/day trend over timestamps.
This detector has no resource-age gate.

## Detection and severity

| Configuration | Default |
|---|---|
| `min_current_bytes` | `1073741824.0` |
| `min_rate_bytes_per_day` | `107374182.4` |
| `horizon_high_days` | `30.0` |
| `horizon_medium_days` | `90.0` |
| `horizon_low_days` | `180.0` |

For a partition below 50 GiB, current size must be at least 1 GiB, fitted growth at least
0.1 GiB/day (102.4 MiB/day), and projected time to 50 GiB at most 180 days.
The finding is High within 30 days, Medium within 90, otherwise Low within 180; endpoints
are inclusive. Below the rate floor or beyond the horizon is stable.

**A partition already at or above 50 GiB is High regardless of growth.** It takes precedence
over future projections on sibling partitions, independent of input order. Otherwise the
detector selects the soonest projected limit; reported projections are rounded.

## Evidence and limits

5 GiB growing at 1.5 GiB/day projects 30 days and is High; 0.5 GiB/day projects 90 days
and is Medium; 0.25 GiB/day projects 180 days and is Low. A flat 50 GiB partition still
uses the urgent override.

No positive current size is insufficient data. A series with fewer than two samples (or no
timestamp spread) has zero fitted growth, not a reliable trend; a single full-size sample
can still trigger the override. Failed reads are errors. There is no guarantee that a linear
trend continues, or that a physical partition cannot split before the projected point.
Physical storage does not prove the size of any individual logical key.
